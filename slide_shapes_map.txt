  Infrastructure Reusability: Leverages existing state GIS portals and open satellite feeds, requiring minimal hardware installation.
  Scalable Deployment: Can be implemented in high-risk pilot districts (e.g. Wayanad, Mandi) and expanded nationwide as needed.
  Cost-Effectiveness: Setup cost is near-zero compared to building physical infrastructure or post-disaster rebuilding.
  Authority Integration: Includes features like SOS alerts, shelter capacity telemetry, and DM manual override for disaster control.

  VIABILITY✔ Proven & Transparent
  Proven Concept: Leverages the success of GIS + MILP optimization in emergency logistics, demonstrating clear operational viability.
  Builds Public Trust: Explainable AI (SHAP) minimizes human bias, while transparent metrics provide credible proof for all evacuation orders.
  Guarantees Accountability: Fosters transparency through detailed logs, shelter manifests, and analytics, ensuring every action is traceable.
  Ensures Authority Engagement: Real-time GIS dashboards and one-click dispatch alerts keep disaster management teams actively in control.
