ResQGrid: AI-Powered Proactive Relocation Intelligence
Revolutionizing India's Disaster Resilience
Real-world issue:
Flash floods, landslides (Wayanad, Himachal), and unplanned evacuations cause massive casualties, shelter bottlenecks, and severe resource wastage.
Why important:
India loses ₹80,000+ crores annually to climate disasters; 30M+ people displaced in a decade. 85% of evacuation failures stem from blind shelter allocation & road cutoffs.
Solution:
GeoAI system identifies dynamic red zones, models shelter carrying capacity, and optimizes preemptive relocation for vulnerable citizens.

FEASIBILITY ANALYSIS

Infrastructure Reusability:

Scalable Deployment:
Can be implemented in high-risk pilot districts (e.g. Wayanad, Mandi) and expanded nationwide as needed.
Cost-Effectiveness:
Setup cost is near-zero compared to building physical infrastructure or post-disaster rebuilding.
Authority Integration:
Includes features like SOS alerts, shelter capacity telemetry, and DM manual override for disaster control.
VIABILITY
✔ Proven & Transparent
Proven Concept:
Leverages the success of GIS + MILP optimization in emergency logistics, demonstrating clear operational viability.
Builds Public Trust:
Explainable AI (SHAP) minimizes human bias, while transparent metrics provide credible proof for all evacuation orders.

Fosters transparency through detailed logs, shelter manifests, and analytics, ensuring every action is traceable.
